The filings include beneficial ownership misrepresentations (Schedule 13D/G), 40 Sarbanes-Oxley certifications signed under penalty of perjury attesting to financial statements Cane knew to be false (25 Section 302, 15 Section 906), and annual/quarterly reports that concealed the family bloc's control, the CEDE \& CO. accumulation, the offshore nominee structure, and all related-party transactions. Each filing was a discrete act of wire fraud. These 310 SEC filings represent only the EDGAR-transmitted subset of the enterprise's false representations to government agencies; they do not include the false court filings, bankruptcy schedules, tax returns, unreported FBARs, and other regulatory submissions documented elsewhere in this analysis.

Cane continues to commit wire fraud in furtherance of the enterprise's concealment.
