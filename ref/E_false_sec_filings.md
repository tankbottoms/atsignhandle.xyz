# Appendix E: Accession Documents with False Statements

This appendix catalogs every SEC filing containing material misrepresentations by Kyleen Elisabeth Cane (f/k/a Michael Anthony Cane) and Jan Mary Wallace (a/k/a Juanita Mary Jardine) across five CIK entities controlled or serviced by the Cane-Wallace enterprise. Each filing constitutes a separate violation of federal securities law. Comprehensive programmatic scanning of all CIK filing archives identified **31 false filings** containing identity fraud, false SOX certifications, and concealed related-party transactions.

---

## V. Analysis: Pattern of False Statements

### A. Identity Fraud as Securities Fraud Infrastructure

The 1,120-day concealment of Cane's legal name change was not administrative negligence. During this period, Cane simultaneously signed SEC filings under the former identity, operated the Cane O'Neill attorney trust account under the former identity in the Thomas & Wong fraud, and signed corporate documents for MW Fitness and Savannah Corp as "Michael Cane."[^23] The identity concealment served a strategic purpose: it severed the paper trail connecting "Kyleen Cane" (the post-merger majority shareholder) to "Michael Cane" (Dynamic Associates' securities attorney who negotiated the merger on behalf of both parties), thereby obscuring the conflict of interest at the heart of the reverse merger.

[^23]: *See* Forensic Brief 16, Section IV (cross-CIK analysis documenting identity fraud across all four entity families).

### B. Statutory Violations

Each false SEC filing constitutes a separate violation under the following statutes:

- **15 U.S.C. Section 78j(b) / 17 C.F.R. Section 240.10b-5**: Securities fraud through material misrepresentation of the certifier's identity in connection with the purchase or sale of securities.

- **17 C.F.R. Section 240.13d-2(a)**: Failure to promptly amend Schedule 13D upon a material change (legal name change of the reporting person). The 1,120-day delay constitutes 17 separate quarterly reporting cycles during which the identity change went unreported.

- **18 U.S.C. Section 1350**: False SOX certifications. The LATI 10-QSB SOX certification signed as "Michael A. Cane" on December 13, 2002 constitutes a willful certification of a periodic report that did not comply with SEC requirements, as the identity of the certifier was false. Wallace's two MW Medical SOX certifications (November 19, 2002 and June 30, 2003) concealed her use of fraudulent Social Security Numbers and offshore nominee accounts. Willful violations carry penalties of up to $5,000,000 and 20 years imprisonment per count.

- **18 U.S.C. Section 1028A**: Aggravated identity theft. Use of the former legal identity "Michael A. Cane" during the commission of wire fraud (trust account scheme) and securities fraud (false SEC filings) constitutes aggravated identity theft carrying a mandatory consecutive 2-year sentence per count.
