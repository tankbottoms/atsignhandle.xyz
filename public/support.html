Downloads and delivery

Everything we sell is digital; nothing ships. A download link appears on the confirmation page and is emailed to you, normally within seconds and always within one hour. Purchases stay re-downloadable indefinitely — you do not lose a file by losing the email.

  • No email arrived — check spam, then write to us with the purchase address. We will resend the link.
  • Link expired — links are individually signed and time-limited for security. Ask and we will issue a fresh one.
  • File will not open — tell us the filename and the application you opened it with. Vector files (.eps) need Illustrator, Affinity Designer, Inkscape, or similar; they are not images and will not open in Preview or Photos.
