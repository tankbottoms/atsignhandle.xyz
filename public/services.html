How an engagement runs

1. Describe the work. Write to us with what you want built or drawn, roughly when you need it, and any budget ceiling. A ceiling is useful information, not a bargaining position — it decides scope.

2. Scope and quote. We come back with a written scope: what is included, what is not, the deliverable formats, who owns what, and either an hourly estimate or a fixed fee. This is free and carries no obligation.

3. Schedule. Once the quote is agreed we book the hours against a start date and tell you honestly what the queue looks like.

4. Build and invoice. Work proceeds against the scope with checkpoints where you can redirect it. Invoices are issued as work completes and charged through Stripe, appearing on your statement as ATSIGNHANDLE.XYZ.

Rates and scheduling

Rates depend on the discipline and on how much of the risk sits with us — hourly against an estimate is priced differently from a fixed fee for a fixed scope, and exclusive or work-for-hire rights on illustration are priced differently from a licence.

Ask and we will send the current rate card with the quote. There is no charge for asking and no obligation to proceed.
